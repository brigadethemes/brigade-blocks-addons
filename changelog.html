    Changelog

    What changed in each release. The same notes appear on the plugin’s WordPress.org page.

    Version 1.0.0

    • Styling system: per-element, per-state and responsive styling controls for every block, in the native Styles tab with live editor preview.
    • New Container block: flex/grid layout types, boxed content width, presets, flex-item settings for any child block, positioning, transforms, responsive visibility.
    • Styling system: layout, sizing, position, transform and transition-timing controls; breakpoints filterable with wpbb_style_breakpoints.
    • Initial release: modular core, settings screen, Donix design tokens, Content (14 blocks), Interactive (6 blocks), Marketing (4 blocks), Posts (Posts block with five layouts, Author Box, Dynamic Content) and Media (Gallery, Before / After, Video Gallery) modules.
    • WooCommerce module: Products, Product Categories, Product Search, Add to Cart, Mini Cart, Store Page.
    • Forms & Users module: Login Form (login, registration, lost password, without page reloads), User Profile, Form Styler for Contact Form 7 and WPForms.
    • Advanced module: Advanced Search with live results, Taxonomy Filter.
    • Slider block: slides you design yourself with any blocks, each with its own background image, colour and overlay; slide or fade effect, one or several slides at a time, arrows, dots and autoplay.
    • Settings screen: saves without reloading the page; modules and blocks of the Pro add-on are marked PRO and listed as locked while Pro is not active.
    • Data Visualization module: Chart (bar, horizontal bar, line, area, pie, doughnut, radar; typed, pasted or from a CSV / Google Sheets link), Progress Chart, Statistics Cards.

    Coming next

    Pricing Table, Countdown and Logo Showcase, then People (team members, testimonials), Navigation (breadcrumbs, table of contents) and Utilities (social icons, WhatsApp chat) — see the Roadmap.
