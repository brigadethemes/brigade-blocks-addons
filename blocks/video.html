    Content module

    Video block

    YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted video. External players load only when clicked, keeping pages fast and privacy-friendly.

    Overview

    The Video block plays a YouTube or Vimeo video, or a video file from your Media Library. By default it shows a poster image with a play button and loads the YouTube or Vimeo player only when the visitor presses play (“click to load”). This keeps pages fast and avoids sending visitor data to YouTube or Vimeo until they choose to watch. YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, and Vimeo uses “Do Not Track”.

    At a glance

    Block name
    wpbb/video
    Module
    Content
    Find it by searching
    Video
    Loads JavaScript on the site
    Yes, a small script, only on pages that use this block

    When and why to use it

    • Product demos, testimonials and explainer videos.
    • Background-style muted, looping clips (with autoplay).
    • Videos hosted on your own site.

    Example scenarios

    • Homepage: a YouTube product tour with a custom poster image.
    • Hero: a short self-hosted clip, autoplaying muted and looping, without controls.

    How to add the Video block

    1. Open the editor. In your WordPress dashboard go to Pages (or Posts) and click the page you want to edit, or click Add New. For headers, footers and templates, use Appearance → Editor (block themes).
    2. Add a new block. Click the blue + (Block Inserter) button in the top-left corner, or the small + that appears where you want the block. You can also type / on an empty line.
    3. Search for the block. Type Video in the search field. Brigade Blocks appear in the Brigade Blocks category and show the coral block icon.
    4. Insert it. Click Video in the results. The block appears with a field for the video address.
    5. Configure the content. Edit the text directly on the block, then open the Settings tab (gear icon) in the right-hand sidebar for the options described below. If the sidebar is hidden, click the gear icon in the top-right corner.
    6. Customise the design. Switch to the Styles tab (half-filled circle icon) to change colours, spacing, borders and more — see Design & styling.
    7. Save or publish. Click Save draft to keep working later, Preview to check it, or Publish / Update to make it live.

    Content settings

    You set up this block in two places: on the block itself in the editor, and in the Settings tab (gear icon) of the sidebar on the right. Every option is listed below, with its default value.

    On the block (in the editor canvas)

    Video URL

    What it does
    The video to play.
    What to enter or choose
    A YouTube link (watch, youtu.be, shorts, live or embed links), a Vimeo link, or a video file ending in .mp4, .webm, .ogv, .ogg, .mov or .m4v. Or click Upload or choose from Media Library.
    Default
    Empty
    How to use it
    Paste a full address, including https://.
    When to use it
    Always.
    Example
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ.
    Result
    The video preview appears. Unrecognised addresses show the message “Unrecognised URL…”.
    Good to know
    If the address is empty or not recognised, nothing is shown on the site.

    Settings panel: Video

    Accessible title

    What it does
    A description of the video for screen-reader users.
    What to enter or choose
    A short title.
    Default
    Empty
    How to use it
    Type into the text field. Changes appear in the preview right away.
    When to use it
    Always.
    Example
    “Product tour: creating your first project”.
    Result
    Used as the player’s title. Defaults to “Video”.

    Aspect ratio

    What it does
    The shape of the player.
    What to enter or choose
    One of: 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, 21:9.
    Default
    16:9
    How to use it
    Open the dropdown and pick an option.
    When to use it
    Match the video: 16:9 for most, 9:16 for vertical phone videos, 21:9 for cinematic ones.
    Example
    9:16 for a YouTube Short.
    Result
    The player area changes shape.

    Rounded corners

    What it does
    Rounds the player corners.
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    On
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    Example
    On for the Donix look.
    Result
    Corners change.

    Settings panel: Playback

    Load player on click

    What it does
    Shows a poster and play button, and loads YouTube or Vimeo only when played.
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    On
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    When to use it
    Keep it on (faster, more private). Turn off only if you need the full player immediately.
    Example
    On for all YouTube embeds.
    Result
    On: a poster with a play button; the player loads on click and starts playing.
    Good to know
    Ignored when Autoplay is on.

    Autoplay (muted)

    What it does
    Starts the video automatically, muted (browsers only allow muted autoplay).
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    Off
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    When to use it
    For short decorative clips.
    Example
    A silent looping hero clip.
    Result
    The video plays as soon as the page loads, without sound.

    Muted

    What it does
    Starts without sound.
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    Off
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    Example
    For background clips.
    Result
    Visitors can unmute with the controls.

    Loop

    What it does
    Restarts the video when it ends.
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    Off
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    Example
    Short ambient clips.
    Result
    Plays continuously.

    Show controls

    What it does
    Shows the player’s play, volume and progress controls.
    What to enter or choose
    On or off.
    Default
    On
    How to use it
    Click the switch to turn it on (blue) or off.
    When to use it
    Keep on for normal videos; off only for decorative muted loops.
    Example
    Off for a background clip.
    Result
    Controls hide.

    Settings panel: Poster image

    Poster image

    What it does
    The picture shown before the video plays.
    What to enter or choose
    An image from the Media Library. Choose poster, Replace poster or Remove poster.
    Default
    0
    How to use it
    Click the button to open the Media Library, then upload a new file or pick an existing one and click “Select”.
    When to use it
    To show a branded image instead of the video’s own thumbnail.
    Example
    A still with your logo.
    Result
    Shown before playback. Without one, YouTube videos use their own thumbnail (loaded from i.ytimg.com).

    Design & styling

    Select the block, open the Styles tab in the settings sidebar (the half-filled circle icon next to the gear) and use the three pickers at the top of the Style panel:

    1. Element — which part of the block to style.
    2. State — Normal, or a state such as Hover (only the states the part supports are offered).
    3. Device — Desktop, Tablet or Mobile, for values that can differ per screen size.

    Responsive settings

    Brigade Blocks uses three screen sizes: Desktop (wider than 1023px), Tablet (600–1023px) and Mobile (599px and narrower). Tablet and Mobile values are optional: when empty, the value of the next larger screen is used.

    In the Styles tab, pick the Device first; these controls can then have a different value for that screen size: Radius, Margin, Width, Max width, Height, Min width / Min height / Max height, Block alignment, Position, Top / Right / Bottom / Left, Z-index, Overflow.

    The Visibility panel (Styles tab, Normal state) can hide the block entirely on Desktop, Tablet or Mobile.

    Advanced options

    • HTML anchor (Advanced section of the Settings tab) — a unique name such as pricing, so you can link straight to this block with #pricing at the end of the page address (for example from a menu).
    • Additional CSS class(es) (Advanced section) — for custom CSS from your theme or the Site Editor. Separate several classes with spaces.
    • Custom attributes (Advanced section) — add data-*, title, aria-label, aria-describedby, lang or dir attributes. See Custom attributes.
    • State transition (Styles tab → Effects, Normal state) — how smoothly hover and focus styles animate.
    • Width alignment (block toolbar) — Wide width, Full width, when your theme supports them.

    How it works on your site

    With “Load player on click”, visitors first see the poster and a play button; clicking it replaces the poster with the player (a tiny script does this). Video files use the browser’s built-in player.

    Accessibility

    • Give every video an Accessible title.
    • Avoid autoplay for videos with sound or important content; if you use autoplay, keep controls on so visitors can pause it.
    • Provide captions in the video itself (YouTube/Vimeo) for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers.

    Limitations and notes

    • Only YouTube, Vimeo and direct video files are supported.
    • Autoplay is always muted (a browser rule).

    Troubleshooting

    The video does not show on the site.

    Check the address: it must be a YouTube or Vimeo link or a video file. Private YouTube videos cannot be embedded.
