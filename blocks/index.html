    Blocks overview

    Every block in the plugin, grouped by module. Click a block for its complete guide.

    Brigade Blocks Addons adds 56 blocks in 12 modules. 6 of them are “inner” blocks that only live inside another block (for example an Accordion Item inside an Accordion). Every block has its own guide.

    All blocks appear in the block inserter under the Brigade Blocks category. You can switch modules and individual blocks on or off on the Brigade Blocks screen in your dashboard.

    Content module

    Essential blocks for page content: headings, text, images, icons, buttons, video, maps and embeds.

    • Container

      A flexible layout box for other blocks: rows, columns and grids, full-width or boxed content, backgrounds, overlays and per-device layouts.

    • Heading

      A section heading with an optional eyebrow label above it, an accent line and a supporting paragraph below.

    • Text Editor

      Rich text — paragraphs, lists, quotes, headings and images — in the Donix body style, with optional columns and a drop cap.

    • Image

      A responsive image with aspect-ratio cropping, hover effects, rounded corners, a caption and an optional link.

    • Image Box

      An image card with a title, text and link — stacked, or with the text over the image on a dark scrim.

    • Icon

      A single line icon — plain or inside a Donix tinted badge — with an optional link.

    • Icon Box

      A feature or service card: tinted icon badge, title, description and an optional link.

    • Button

      A call-to-action button in the Donix style — primary, dark, outline or text link — with an optional icon.

    • Divider

      A horizontal line with style, weight and width options, and an optional centred label or icon.

    • Spacer

      Empty vertical space with separate heights for desktop, tablet and mobile.

    • Video

      YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted video. External players load only when clicked, keeping pages fast and privacy-friendly.

    • Google Maps

      A Google Map for any address — no API key needed — with an optional info card, directions link, grayscale style and click-to-load privacy mode.

    • HTML

      Custom HTML with a live preview in the editor.

    • Shortcode

      Runs a shortcode from any plugin or theme, with spacing and text styling controls.

    Interactive module

    Sliders you design yourself, accordions, tabs, content toggles, timelines and process steps.

    • Accordion

      Collapsible sections (FAQs, details, specifications) built on the browser’s native disclosure element — with optional FAQ schema for search engines.

    • Accordion Item

      One collapsible section of an Accordion: a title and any blocks as content.

      Inside: Accordion

    • Tabs

      Accessible tabs — underline, pills or boxed, horizontal or vertical — with full keyboard support and links that open a specific tab.

    • Tab

      One panel of a Tabs block, with its title, optional icon and any blocks inside.

      Inside: Tabs

    • Slider

      A slider you design yourself: every slide holds any blocks and has its own background image, colour and overlay. Slide or fade, one or several slides at a time.

    • Slide

      One slide of a Slider. Holds any blocks; its background image, colour, gradient and overlay are set in its Styles tab.

      Inside: Slider

    • Content Toggle

      Switch between two sets of content — like monthly and yearly pricing — with a switch or a segmented control.

    • Toggle Panel

      One side of a Content Toggle. Holds any blocks.

      Inside: Content Toggle

    • Timeline

      A vertical timeline of milestones — one-sided or alternating left and right — with dot, number or icon markers.

    • Timeline Item

      One milestone of a Timeline: a date, a title and any content.

      Inside: Timeline

    • Process Steps

      A row of numbered or icon steps joined by a connector line — explain how you work in a glance.

    • Process Step

      One step of a Process Steps block: marker, title and a short description.

      Inside: Process Steps

    Marketing module

    Conversion-focused blocks for landing pages and business sites.

    • Call to Action

      A conversion banner — eyebrow, headline, supporting text and buttons — on a dark, coral, tinted or image background.

    • Counter

      An animated statistic that counts up when scrolled into view — with prefix, suffix, decimals and an optional icon badge.

    • Flip Box

      A card that flips on hover, keyboard focus or tap to reveal a message and a button.

    • Progress Bar

      A labelled progress or skill bar that fills when scrolled into view.

    Posts module

    Post grids, lists, masonry, carousels and sliders with AJAX load more and filters; author box and dynamic fields.

    • Posts

      Posts from any public post type in a grid, list, masonry, carousel or slider — with “Load more”, infinite scroll and category filter buttons.

    • Author Box

      The post author’s avatar, name, biography, website and a link to all their posts — or any chosen author.

    • Dynamic Content

      Shows one field of the current post — title, excerpt, dates, author, terms, comments, reading time or a custom field. Built for templates and the Query Loop.

    Media module

    Galleries (grid, masonry, carousel, slider) with lightbox and filters, before/after comparisons and video galleries.

    • Gallery

      Images in a grid, masonry, carousel or slider — with a lightbox, captions, hover effects and a filter bar built from image tags.

    • Before / After

      Compare two images with a draggable divider — mouse, touch and keyboard friendly.

    • Video Gallery

      YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted videos in a grid or carousel. Videos open in a lightbox or play in place — and load only when clicked.

    People module

    Team member cards with social links, and testimonials with ratings in a grid or carousel.

    • Team Member

      A person’s photo, name, role, short bio and social links — stacked, side by side, or with the name over the photo.

    • Testimonials

      What your customers say: quotes with name, role, photo and star rating, in a grid or a carousel.

    Navigation module

    Breadcrumbs with structured data, and a table of contents that builds itself from your headings.

    • Breadcrumbs

      Shows where the visitor is — Home › Category › Post — and builds itself for every page, post, archive and search, with breadcrumb structured data for search engines.

    • Table of Contents

      A list of the headings in your post that jumps to each section. Builds and updates itself, and highlights the section being read.

    WooCommerce module

    Products (grid, list, carousel, slider), product categories, product search, add to cart, a mini cart drawer and cart / checkout / account pages. Requires WooCommerce — these blocks only appear while WooCommerce is active.

    • Products

      WooCommerce products in a grid, list, carousel or slider — latest, featured, on sale, best selling, top rated, hand-picked, related, upsells or cross-sells.

    • Product Categories

      WooCommerce product categories as image tiles in a grid or carousel.

    • Add to Cart

      An add-to-cart button with quantity and price, for any product or the product being viewed.

    • Mini Cart

      A cart icon with item count and total that opens a cart drawer or links to the cart — ideal for headers.

    • Product Search

      A WooCommerce product search box with an optional category dropdown.

    • Store Page

      WooCommerce’s cart, checkout or My Account placed anywhere in your own page layout.

    Forms module

    Login, registration and lost-password forms that work without reloading, a user profile card, and a styler for Contact Form 7 and WPForms.

    • Form Styler

      Shows a Contact Form 7 or WPForms form, styled to match your design.

    • Login Form

      Log in, register and reset a password on your own page — without reloading, and with WordPress’s standard login as a fallback.

    • User Profile

      The logged-in visitor’s avatar, name and bio with account and log-out links — or a login link for logged-out visitors.

    Advanced module

    Advanced Search with live results, and a Taxonomy Filter that links to category, tag and other taxonomy archives.

    • Advanced Search

      A search box with post type and taxonomy filters, and live results as you type.

    • Taxonomy Filter

      Categories, tags or any taxonomy as chips, a list or a dropdown — linking to their archives and highlighting the current one.

    Utilities module

    Social icons for 28 networks, and a WhatsApp chat button with a greeting card.

    • Social Icons

      Links to your social profiles as icons: 28 networks, in brand colours or your own style, with optional names.

    • WhatsApp Chat

      Lets visitors message you on WhatsApp: a floating chat button with an optional greeting card, or a button in your content.

    Data Visualization module

    Bar, line, area, pie, doughnut and radar charts (typed in or from a CSV / Google Sheets link), progress rings and gauges, and statistics cards — lightweight SVG with accessible data tables.

    • Chart

      Bar, horizontal bar, line, area, pie, doughnut or radar charts — from numbers you type or a CSV / Google Sheets link. Lightweight SVG, readable by screen readers, no chart library.

    • Progress Chart

      One or more progress rings or half-circle gauges with the value in the middle.

    • Statistics Cards

      Key numbers as cards — each with a label, a count-up value, a trend badge and an optional sparkline.
